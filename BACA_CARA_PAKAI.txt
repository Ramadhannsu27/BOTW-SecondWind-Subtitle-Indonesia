PENGATURAN WAJIB DI CEMU 2 AGAR TEKS INDONESIA AKTIF:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Buka Cemu 2.
2. Masuk ke: Options -> General settings -> General tab.
3. Pastikan Console Language diatur ke "English".
4. Masuk ke: Options -> Graphic Packs -> The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild -> Mods.
5. Berikan tanda centang pada:
   [v] Second Wind
   [v] BOTW & Second Wind - Subtitle Bahasa Indonesia by Ramadhannsu
6. Mainkan game!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CARA 1: INSTALASI OTOMATIS (REKOMENDASI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Klik 2x file: "Pasang_Ke_Cemu.bat"
2. Masukkan lokasi folder Cemu 2 Anda (misalnya: C:\Cemu atau D:\Games\Cemu)
3. Tekan Enter. Script akan otomatis memasang Graphic Pack ke Cemu.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CARA 2: INSTALASI MANUAL (COPY - PASTE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy folder mod ini.
2. Masuk ke folder instalasi Cemu 2 Anda -> buka folder "graphicPacks".
3. Tempel (Paste) folder ke dalam "graphicPacks".
   Strukturnya:
   Cemu/graphicPacks/BOTW_SecondWind_Indo_GraphicPack/
       ├── rules.txt
       └── content/
           ├── Pack/
           │   └── Bootup_USen.pack
           └── System/
               └── Resource/
                   └── ResourceSizeTable.product.srsizetable
